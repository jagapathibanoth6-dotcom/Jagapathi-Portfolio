IoT

Smart Starter & Transformer Gateway — Connected Motor Control

Upcoming · Starter PCBs designed, prototypes next

A two-part system for farms that share a distribution transformer. Smart starters at each motor talk by LoRa radio to one gateway at the transformer, which monitors the supply, connects to the internet and lets farmers control their motors from a mobile app.

Part 1 · Smart Starter (each motor)

Switches, protects and measures a three-phase motor: voltage, current, power, power factor, phase loss and phase sequence. ESP32-S3 controller, display and controls, 5 kV isolation and a fail-safe contactor drive. Controller and measurement PCBs are routed and checked; next are manufacturing files and three prototypes.

Part 2 · Transformer Gateway (one per transformer)

Measures transformer voltage and phase currents, analyses the load, and connects to the internet through a single SIM. Sends commands to and receives status from every starter within about 5 km, and pushes live updates to the app.

  • Cost-efficient: one SIM and internet connection serves 5–30 motors, instead of one per starter
  • Protects the transformer: starts motors in sequence, one by one, to avoid the load surge that damages transformers (a government concern)
  • Two ways to control: a mobile app over the internet, and a remote control that works directly by radio within a 5 km range; both start, stop and monitor motors, with fault and supply-loss alerts

Skills: Product & system design · IoT architecture (LoRa + cellular) · PCB design · Electrical safety · Load management · Embedded systems (ESP32) · Test planning
